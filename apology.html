♡ A LETTER FROM MY HEART ♡ FOR YOU

I'm Sorry, Bebi

I'm sorry for having these types of issues sa akong self po. I'm just so scared nga mawalaa jud kas akoa completely.

Even sa katong times nga magbuot kos imong suoton or what, I will no longer do it anymore since karon kabalo naman pud ka mo control sa sakto ug dili. I'll trust you on that.

I'm so sorry nga grabe akong pagka seloso and pagka overprotective :>. As for my overthinking, mostly nag-cause ani kay akoa rang pagka seloso. Gusto raman jud nako ug assurance :>. I'm sorry if ing-ana ko po. That's just me being myself, and I know I still have to work on that part of me.

And for my nightmares, I keep having these nightmares man gud about you and me... nga naa juy some random guy nga mokuha nimo away from me. I really don't know nganong this keeps happening po.

But karon as I'm making this message, nawala siya. The last occurrence ani kay last week Monday until Thursday po huhu. I'm really sorry.

And as for my clinginess out in public, I know nga hate nimo ang PDA. I'm sorry nga we had times nga clingy ra kaayo ko nimo gawas. I hope you can forgive me for everything nga akong sayop nga nabuhat :>.

And I hope nga sometime puhon, if okay ra nimo, maybe sa time nga magkuyog man gani ta wherever fate takes us, like katong mag-samgyup tang kyuti...

I hope nga okay ra nimo nga mokupot ko sa imong hands? Please? Bisag kana lang. I'll do my best to resist kissing your forehead, unless you're allowing me to :>. I'd be happy.

And thank you for all the sacrifices that you made for me, Alexa. Don't ever think nga none of it mattered, okay? All of them mattered to me. From the smallest to the biggest sacrifices, I acknowledge and appreciate every one of them.

And please, just give me time and patience lang to work on my issues and to keep improving myself for you. I know I still have things to learn, and I don't expect everything to change overnight.

I just want you to know that I'm trying. Not because I want to become someone I'm not, but because I want to become someone who can love you without letting my fears get in the way.

Thank you for being patient with me.

I love you, bebi. ♡

— from the person still learning
