To You, From My Heart

For the girl who became a beautiful part of my story

♡

Hi, love.

I don't really know how to start this without making everything sound too complicated, so maybe I'll just say what I've always wanted you to know. Thank you. Thank you for coming into my life, for letting me know you, and for allowing me to become someone who could love you this deeply. Sa tanan nga memories nato, even the simple ones, naa gyud something about them that I don't want to forget. The random conversations, the laughs, the little moments, and the times nga wala ta nag-expect nga mahimong special pero somehow became some of my favorite memories.

And honestly, ikaw gyud. Out of all the people I could have met, somehow it was you. The girl who once thought I wouldn't last. The girl who had her walls up. The girl who thought maybe someday I'd get tired and leave. But I stayed because you were worth knowing. You were worth understanding. And somewhere along the way, without me even realizing it, you became someone I cared about more than I knew how to explain.

That's why I'm sorry. I'm sorry for the things I said when I was overwhelmed. I'm sorry for the times when my fear became louder than my love. I'm sorry for making decisions while my mind was full of "what ifs" instead of talking to you and letting you know what was really happening inside my head.

There were times nga I was scared. Not because I didn't love you, but because I was scared of losing myself. I was scared that maybe someday things would change, that maybe your feelings would change, and I'd end up hurting more than I could handle. And maybe, without realizing it, I tried to protect myself from a pain that hadn't even happened yet.

I left first because I was afraid of being left later. And looking back, I wish I had just told you that. I wish I had said, "I'm scared." I wish I had told you that I was confused, that I didn't know how to handle everything I was feeling, instead of making you feel like leaving was the answer. I'm sorry for that.

But I also want you to know something. I don't want this letter to be another promise filled with big words. I don't want to tell you that everything will magically become perfect, because I know that's not how love works. What I want is something much simpler. I want to learn. I want to understand you better. I want to communicate instead of assuming. I want to listen instead of letting my thoughts create stories that aren't even real. I want to be honest when I'm scared instead of running away from something just because I'm afraid of how it might end.

And if we ever get another chance to build something together, I don't want us to simply go back to what we had. I want us to create something better. Something where we can talk about the things that hurt. Something where we don't have to threaten to leave whenever things get difficult. Something where both of us can still have our own lives, our own space, and our own dreams while choosing each other at the same time.

Because loving someone shouldn't mean losing yourself. It should mean becoming better while still being yourself.

And I want you to know that you don't have to figure everything out right now. If you're scared, okay ra. If you're unsure, okay ra. If there are still questions in your heart, I understand. I don't expect you to suddenly forget everything that happened just because I'm here again. You have every right to be careful with your heart.

So if there's one thing I want you to feel from this little website, this letter, and everything inside this box, it's not pressure. It's reassurance. You are loved. You are appreciated. You are remembered. And you matter to me more than I sometimes knew how to show.

And please, amping sa imong heart, okay? You've been through enough. You don't have to pretend nga you're okay all the time. You don't have to be strong every second. If someday things feel heavy, I hope you remember that you don't always have to carry everything alone.

I can't promise you that I'll never make mistakes again, but I can promise that I want to learn from them. I can't promise that our future will always be easy, but I can promise that if we choose to walk forward together, I don't want to run away just because things become difficult. And I can't promise you a perfect story, but maybe we don't need one.

Maybe we just need something real. Something honest. Something patient. Something that we both choose, even on the days when choosing each other takes a little more effort.

So, whatever happens next, thank you for being you. Thank you for the memories. Thank you for the laughter. Thank you for the moments that made ordinary days feel special. Thank you for letting me love you.

And if life gives us another chapter, I hope we write it differently this time—not because our past wasn't beautiful, but because we learned from the parts that hurt. No rushing. No forcing. No pretending. Just us, figuring things out as we go.

Hinay-hinay lang ta.

And if you ever wonder whether everything we had meant something to me, it did. It still does. Because no matter how complicated our story became, one thing has always been simple for me:

I'm grateful that it was you. ♡

Always with a little piece of my heart,

— Yours, ♡
